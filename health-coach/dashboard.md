### Staples (locked understanding)

- **One starch per meal:** ≤2 roti **or** ≤200 g Qilla white rice (Ashirwad chakki atta) — not both.
- **Daal / lobia** = good staples (protein + fiber); not “bad carbs.”
- **Tostas** = rare OK.
- **Haleem** = 1–2×/month — ignore in weekly strategy.
- Oil default: **1–1.5 tbsp/pot**; creep order = starch volume &gt; oil &gt; chai sugar (chai sugar kept).

### Weekly adjust

| If… | Then… |
|---|---|
| Lose ≥0.8–1.0 kg/week | Hold calories & walks |
| Lose &lt;0.5–0.7 for 2 weeks | Tighten starch portions — not protein |
| Lose &gt;1.2–1.5 with fatigue | Bump food slightly |
| Protein ≪ 130 | Add whey or lean meat |
| Intake ≫ 1,800 | Flag day (usually portion, not staple choice) |
