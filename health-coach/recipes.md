# Household recipe book (cheat sheet)

Source: family notes (Roman Urdu), trained 24 Sep 2026.  
Household defaults: pot often feeds **2 adults + toddler** with **50–100% leftovers**.

**Oil (updated Oct 2026):** household uses **~1–1.5 tbsp oil per whole cooking pot** (was ~2 dish spoons ≈ 5–7 tbsp).  
≈ **120–180 kcal oil per pot** (~120 kcal/tbsp), shared across servings + leftovers.  
His typical share of pot oil often ~**30–70 kcal** depending on gravy amount (not counting starch).  
Use midpoint **~1.25 tbsp/pot (~150 kcal oil)** unless photo looks unusually dry/oily.

**Units:** 1 pao ≈ 250 g. Pao chamach ≈ ¼ tsp/tbsp in context.

## Choley chawal
- Brown onion (adhi/pao) hard like matar pulao.
- Sabut garam masala, lehsan-adrak, namak, hari mirch → bhoon.
- Add choley → bhoon → add chawal.
- **Log notes:** Rice + chickpeas + browned-onion oil. Starch-heavy. Confirm rice heap on plate.

## Matar chawal
- Brown onion → garam masala, GG → heavy bhoon.
- Fine hari mirch → splash water → paste.
- Matar → short bhoon → soften → fill water → boil → add chawal.
- **Log notes:** Rice + peas + onion oil. Similar to choley chawal without choley calories.

## Aloo ki qatliyan
- Oil → zeera → potato slices. **No water.**
- Sabut + crushed mirch, haldi, salt, little imli → dum.
- **Log notes:** Oil-cooked potatoes (absorbs fat). No roti stacking if rice elsewhere. Ask how oily / how many slices.

## Khara qeema
- 1 onion light pink → qeema → dry meat water → GG → bhoon.
- Kuti mirch, 1 cut sabut mirch, sabut garam masala, haldi, namak.
- 2 tomatoes → yoghurt → bhoon → 6–8 hari mirch → fine ginger on top → bhoon. **No water at all.**
- **Log notes:** Beef/chicken qeema (ask which). Yoghurt + dry bhoon = moderate fat. Typical with 1–2 roti.

## Aloo matar gajar
- Brown onion → soften tomatoes → GG.
- ~¼ tsp each pisi + kuti mirch, little haldi → bhoon.
- Gajar + aloo + matar → bhoon → water → simmer soft.
- **Log notes:** Veg-forward; oil mainly from onion bhoon. Lower cal than meat salans if oil measured.

## Bhindi
- Half onion to **gulaabi** (not very brown) → little zeera → bhindi.
- **No water.** Sabut or kuti mirch → bhoon → 5–6 imli grains → salt only when soft, before off.
- **Log notes:** Dry bhindi can soak oil. Ask ladle oiliness / how much bhindi on plate.

## Red beans (same method as khari masur)
- Mirch, haldi, namak, GG, ground dhaniya → cook.
- Mirch = dhaniya qty, not too much.
- Finish: **lehsun ka baghaar**.
- **Log notes:** Beans + garlic baghaar oil spike at end. Ask baghaar heaviness.

## Haleem (large batch)
1. **Chicken stock:** 1–1.5 kg bone-in chicken, water, sabut kali mirch, sabut lehsan, sabut adrak (or 1 tsp powder), 1 big rough onion → boil soft → shred, keep stock.
2. **Daal mix:** 1 haleem daal packet + 1 tbsp chana dal + 1 tbsp urad dal + 1 tbsp rice → wash → full heat 1–2 h until maleeda → set aside.
3. **Combine:** Big pot → brown onion → full packet masala + 1 tsp qorma masala + little haldi → GG → bhoon → chicken → bhoon → maleeda daal + stock → ghota → texture.
- **Log notes:** Dense cal (chicken + lentils + rice + browned onion oil). Batch is huge — ask **bowl size** (katori vs large). Per-adult share is a fraction of 1–1.5 kg chicken pot.

## Karhi
- 250 g dahi (2 boxes) + **2 heaped tbsp besan** + 1 medium ground onion + haldi, mirch, water → cook.
- Salt at end → add **pakoray** → baghaar: curry leaves, zeera, sabut mirch, lehsan.
- **Log notes:** Yoghurt + besan base; **pakoray + baghaar** drive fat. Ask pakora count in bowl. Related to besan dishes (cf. Paltay/karhi family).

## Chicken salan
- Brown onions → grind, set aside.
- Sabut garam masala → dhaniya + laal mirch + haldi → bhoon → dahi → bhoon → ground onion → bhoon (splash water if dry).
- Chicken + GG → bhoon → when oil separates add water for shorba → medium flame until done; oil rises → off.
- **Log notes:** Classic oily shorba chicken. Ask gravy amount drunk/soaked + roti count. Skinless chicken assumed from household default.

## Makhan wali daal
- Lehsan-adrak, kuti mirch, zeera, chopped green chillies, little haldi.
- Salt at end.
- **Log notes:** Name implies butter — **ask if makhan/butter added** (not in spice list). Otherwise light dal + tadka oil.

## Dahi baray (batter for 8–10 barey)
- 3 tbsp besan + 1–1.25 tbsp maida + ¼ tsp soda.
- Beat well; batter not too thin/thick.
- **Shallow fry only** (not deep fry).
- **Log notes:** ~8–10 pieces total batch. Log by **piece count** + yoghurt topping amount. Shallow fry ≠ zero oil.

## Chicken sticks
- **1 pao (250 g) chicken**.
- Marinade: 2 tbsp sirka, 1 spoon soya, GG, ½ tbsp tandoori, ½ tbsp chicken tikka → 30 min fridge.
- Brush maida slurry **or** egg + breadcrumbs (pepper + salt in crumbs); don’t dunk stick.
- Freeze 5–10 min → **shallow fry**; don’t disturb while frying.
- **Log notes:** Fixed protein base 250 g raw chicken for whole batch — ask how many sticks he ate / share of batch. Breadcrumb + shallow fry adds carbs/fat.

## Banana protein pancakes (batch of 4)
- Source: shared recipe 2026-10-24 · adapted to **Critical Whey** (not Impact)
- **Ingredients (whole batch):** 1 large ripe banana · 2 large eggs · **1 serving Critical Whey** (2 scoops / 33 g — Chocolate or Frappuccino) · **~35 g rolled oats** (1 rounded oat scoop / ~⅓ cup) · pan **1 spray** oil
- Method: blend banana+eggs smooth → add whey+oats → blend · medium heat non-stick · 4 pancakes (~5″) · flip when bubbles
- **Honest macros (batch):** ~**520 kcal · P 43 · C 58 · F 14** (Chocolate whey) · +spray ~5
- **Per pancake (÷4):** ~**130 kcal · P 11 · C 15 · F 4**
- Recipe card claiming **115 kcal / 15 g P each** undercounts protein powder math — use numbers above
- Quick log: `N banana protein pancakes` → N × **130 / 11 / 15 / 4** · full batch `4 pancakes` → **520 / 43 / 58 / 14**
- Cut tip: great breakfast; skip syrup — berries + Canderel if you want sweet

## Logging priorities (from these recipes)
1. **Starch** — chawal dishes, roti on side, haleem density. Now the #1 calorie lever (oil is low).
2. **Oil / baghaar / shallow-fry** — default **1–1.5 tbsp/pot**. Dry dishes (qatliyan, bhindi, khara qeema) still hold more of that oil on the plate.
3. **Batch fraction** — haleem, dahi baray, chicken sticks, karhi pot.
